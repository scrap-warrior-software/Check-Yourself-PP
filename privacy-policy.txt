Check Yourself keeps its data in the browser's own storage. What is stored: the site list and the
settings, the intention text you type, and one record per session (site, start time, minutes chosen,
status). History is capped at 50 entries on the free plan and 500 as a hard ceiling, and you can
delete one entry or clear the whole history from the popup. The site list and settings are mirrored
through Chrome sync storage only on premium, which carries them between your own signed-in devices.

No data is sent to a server we run. The only external contact is the payment provider
extensionpay.com, and only when its slug is configured: the extension asks the provider for a key,
then reads the plan record for that key, which contains your email address and payment status. The
provider handles payment; this extension never sees card data.

We do not use or transfer data for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness or lending, and we do not
sell it. No human reads your data, because there is no server that receives it. Console output is
failure-path only and stays in your own DevTools. Data is removed when you delete it in the popup or
when the extension is uninstalled.

Limited Use: data collected by this extension is used only to provide the single purpose described on
its Chrome Web Store page. Last updated 2026-11-06.
